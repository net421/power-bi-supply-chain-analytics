# Dashboard Design Notes

## Executive
- KPI cards: OTIF %, Fill Rate %, Cost to Serve, Inventory Turns.
- Trend: OTIF YTD vs prior year.
- Gauge: Days of Supply with 15/24/36/45 thresholds.
- Supplier bar: top/bottom performers by OTIF.
- Regional freight view with drill-through.

## Operational
- Late-order exception table with order, supplier, warehouse, promised and delivery dates.
- OTIF heatmap by product category × warehouse.
- Fill Rate vs Days of Supply scatter plot.
- Stockout/root-cause analysis.
- Slicers: date, supplier, warehouse, product.

## Cost Analysis
- Freight budget variance waterfall.
- Cost/kg by transport mode.
- Monthly freight trend with forecast.
- Supplier × lane cost matrix.
- Decomposition tree for total freight cost.

### UX
Use a consistent executive header, 16:9 canvas, limited visual density, clear target/actual comparisons, and tooltip pages for KPI definitions.

### Power BI binary/screenshots
The `.pbix` and rendered PNGs require Power BI Desktop and therefore are not generated by the GitHub connector. This file is the reproducible visual specification.
